# Market Strategy

## Beachhead Market

Primary target:

- Indie niche perfume brands
- Seongsu/Hannam/Yeonnam-style pop-up fragrance brands
- Lifestyle brands that sell scent as experience
- IP collaboration teams for drama, film, game, exhibition, and idol-album fragrance goods

## Core Insight

Fragrance brands should not compete only on ingredients.
They should compete on scenes.

Consumers rarely buy fragrance only because of a note pyramid.
They buy an imagined situation, a social impression, and a version of themselves.

## Problem

Most fragrance copy fails in one of two ways:

1. Too technical: consumers cannot imagine bergamot, iris, musk, ambroxan, or galbanum.
2. Too abstract: poetic copy sounds beautiful but does not explain when or why to wear the scent.

## Strategic Positioning

Scent Myth Engine is not an AI copywriter.
It is a scene-to-scent translation engine.

The product converts:

- notes into sensory clarity
- places into scenes
- emotions into wearable impressions
- brand tone into controlled language
- IP worlds into scent narratives

## Value Proposition

For fragrance brands:

- Faster product-page writing
- Stronger pop-up storytelling
- More shareable scent cards
- Consistent brand tone across product lines
- Safer surrealism for commercial use

For consumers:

- Clearer understanding of when to wear a scent
- More memorable scent identity
- Personalized scent-scene cards

## Initial Product Offer

### B2B Studio

A web studio for brand teams to generate:

- product names
- one-line hooks
- product-detail copy
- pop-up story cards
- Instagram captions
- IP collaboration scent narratives

### Pop-up Kiosk

A lightweight on-site flow:

1. Visitor selects mood, weather, place, and desired impression.
2. Engine maps the input to brand-approved fragrance SKUs.
3. Engine creates a personal scent-scene card.
4. Visitor scans QR, saves card, and tries the recommended scent.

## Differentiation

Generic LLMs generate attractive copy, but they do not preserve fragrance ontology, brand constraints, and commercial readability at the same time.

Scent Myth Engine combines:

- fragrance ontology
- scene ontology
- brand guardrails
- controlled irrational operators
- structured outputs
- evaluation metrics

- Save/export rate above 35%
- Brand reviewer clarity score above 4.0/5
- Commercial readability score above 4.0/5
- Final edit distance 20% lower than generic LLM baseline
- Kiosk completion rate above 70%
